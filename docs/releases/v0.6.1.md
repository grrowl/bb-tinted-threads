# Tinted Threads 0.6.1

This release fixes a bug with selected rows and brings back the unread style.

## Fixes

- **Selected parent rows keep their tint.** A selected thread with sub-threads
  used to turn grey instead of green or red. It now stays in its tint, with a
  stronger fill and edge to show that it is selected. Tinted parent rows that
  stick to the top of the list while you scroll also get a solid background,
  so rows no longer show through them.
- **Style changes apply on plugin reload.** Tint styles now update when the
  plugin reloads. Before, the old styles stayed until you reloaded the window.

## Unread threads

- Unread threads have a subtle blue tint, a blue dot, and a medium weight
  title. The blue dot and title weight are back from 0.5; the tint is new.
  The blue is fainter than the green and red tints, and it only shows on
  threads that are not working or blocked.
