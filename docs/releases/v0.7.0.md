# Tinted Threads 0.7.0

This release brings back the choice of where pinned threads appear, and it
tidies the list menu.

## Pinned threads

- **Choose where pins go.** Under **Organize → Pinned**, pick **At top** or
  **In group**. At top gathers every pinned thread in the Pinned section, as
  before, and it is still the default. In group leaves each pinned thread in
  its own project, section, or machine group. This option was in version 0.5
  and was lost in 0.6.0.
- **Pins lead their group.** With In group, pinned threads sit at the top of
  their group, and the rest keep your normal sort below them. A small pin icon
  on the row shows that the thread is pinned.

## A shorter menu

- **New Display menu.** It sits under Organize. It holds the row options that
  used to be under Organize → Rows, which are the provider icon and the
  subtitle parts, and it also holds Density. Density is no longer a separate
  item in the list menu.

## Row details

- **Workspace labels show in full.** The branch or worktree name under a
  thread title used to be cut off at a fixed width, even when the row had
  room. It now shows in full, and it is the first part to shrink when the row
  fills up. The uncommitted diff always keeps the width of its content.
